About the Brand

"We believe furniture is the silent poetry of a room. Our pieces are crafted not just to occupy space, but to define it."

Founded on the principles of material integrity and architectural proportion, Creatomy bridges traditional timber joinery with German precision engineering. The result is furniture that is both structurally timeless and visually lightweight.

Prateek

Founder & Lead Designer

Prateek established Creatomy with a singular mission: to challenge the conventions of furniture production in India. Balancing structural logic with a deep respect for organic materials, he guides the design language towards clean lines and architectural clarity.

Under his direction, the studio has merged traditional timber seasoning with advanced 5-axis CNC machining, enabling the workshop to execute complex double-curvature designs that were once considered impossible.

Our Production Facility

Spanning state-of-the-art facilities in Bangalore, our production workshop integrates every step of execution under one roof. We manage the timber from its raw state—using temperature-controlled seasoning kilns to prevent warping—down to the final touch-sanding.

Our facility couples skilled hand-carving artisans with modern German computerized routing centers (5-axis CNC), ensuring absolute tolerance precision, structural integrity, and exquisite detail finishing on every bespoke turnkey production run.
