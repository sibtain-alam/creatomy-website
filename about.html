Our Philosophy

The Art of Space

"Design is not what we do. It is the language through which we understand the world."

Creatomy was born from a desire to merge absolute structural integrity with raw artistic poetry. We design and manufacture bespoke furniture, spatial elements, and custom architectural solutions for projects that demand precision, craftsmanship, and a highly refined editorial perspective.

Prateek

Founder & Principal Designer

"We believe that furniture is not merely functional; it is a structural poetry that defines the emotional boundary of a space."

Prateek founded Creatomy with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between architectural precision and artisanal craftsmanship in India. With a background deeply rooted in design engineering and material science, Prateek oversees the conceptual direction of our collections, ensuring every table joint, wardrobe rail, and fabric stitch meets international benchmarks of luxury.

The Production Facility

Our manufacturing and design facility is located in Bangalore, India, and spans over 50,000 square feet of state-of-the-art machinery integrated with traditional hand-craftsmanship workshops.

Equipped with high-precision 5-axis German CNC machines, advanced timber seasoning kilns, and clean-room polishing cabins, we control every single phase of production. This complete vertical integration allows us to build complex, bespoke architectural wardrobes, high-end veneer surfaces, and heavy solid wood joinery with micro-millimeter precision.

We source our raw wood (including European White Oak, American Walnut, and reclaimed Teak) from certified sustainable forests globally, pairing them with premium Italian structural hardware and performance fabrics.
